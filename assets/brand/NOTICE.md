- `obsidian-official.svg` is the unchanged logo downloaded from [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/images/obsidian-logo-gradient.svg). [Brand guidelines](https://obsidian.md/brand) apply. The Obsidian name and logo are trademarks of their owner; their use identifies compatible tools.
- `character-v1.png` and `character-dark-v1.png` are original generated black-and-white character illustrations. Both are used without image processing.
